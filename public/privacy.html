Website privacy

A small website with no account required.

Updated 9 October 2026

Scope

This notice describes the Atomic.Dev portfolio website. The Android app and any future app features have their own data practices; this notice does not claim to describe them.

What the website collects

This website has no accounts, contact forms, advertising SDKs, or analytics scripts. It does not set application cookies or use browser storage. You can browse the apps without signing in.

Hosting and request information

The website is hosted by Vercel. Hosting and security services may process your IP address, browser information, requested URLs, and request times to deliver the site and help prevent abuse. The website itself does not store a user database. See Vercel’s privacy notice for its practices.

If you contact me

Email and community messages may contain information you choose to share. I use it to respond to your question or feedback. Please avoid sending passwords, API keys, or other sensitive information.

Links to other services

Download, community, social, and support links take you to external services such as GitHub and Discord. Their policies apply after you leave this website.

Questions and requests

For questions about this website or information you have sent directly to the developer, contact testchannelforme@gmail.com. Please describe your request without including credentials.
