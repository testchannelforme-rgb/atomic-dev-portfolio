About the website

Atomic.Dev is an independent developer portfolio. It describes current apps and projects in development and links to external downloads and communities.

App availability

GridHQ links to its current public Android release on GitHub. Still and LEFT are in development. Their interface previews are design concepts and may change. Displayed championship and budget numbers are examples, not user data.

Reviews

The reviews shown are excerpts from community messages supplied by the developer. They describe individual experiences with GridHQ and do not guarantee results for every user. No numerical star ratings have been inferred from the messages.

You may browse and share links to this website. Please do not attempt to interfere with its availability, access information without authorisation, impersonate the developer, or misrepresent the projects.

External services

GitHub downloads, Discord, social pages, and support services are operated separately. Review the applicable terms and app information when using them.
